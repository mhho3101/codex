## Routing reminders

- Hook weak → opening script / first visual / first-three-second sound only.
- Subject missing → full subject reveal, title treatment, and cover; keep approved narration unless terminology changed.
- Voice robotic → audition first; replace narration only after selection.
- Sound reference supplied → copy rhythm and restraint, not the exact sound file.
- Terminology correction → update script, captions, timeline titles, cover, filename, and publish copy together.
- Moderation issue → inspect title, claims, source notes, AI labels, and third-party brand usage before changing visual polish.
